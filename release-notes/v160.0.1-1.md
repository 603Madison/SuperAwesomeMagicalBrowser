([MFSA 2026-130](https://www.mozilla.org/security/advisories/mfsa2026-130/), impact high):

- CVE-2026-130021: a use-after-free in audio and video playback.
- CVE-2026-130020: privilege escalation in WebGL on Android.

1.11.0 and Robolectric 4.18 for its tests.

160.0.1, but GeckoView keeps the major and minor version for dot releases and differs only in the
