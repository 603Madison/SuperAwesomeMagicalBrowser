The first SuperAwesomeMagical Browser: Firefox 160.0 in the family's look.

- Tabs down the left side, with the page as a rounded card beside them.
- Lexend everywhere: the interface, Settings and Firefox's other pages, the start page, and pages
  that don't choose a font.
- The SuperAwesomeMagical colours (aurora purple and green over slate), light or dark with the
  system.
- A start page like the sign-in screen: a big clock, search and your most visited sites over the
  northern lights.
- Ecosia as the search engine.
- No telemetry, studies, sponsored shortcuts or stories.
- Its own profile, apart from any other Firefox.

**Install:** the `.rpm` on Fedora-based systems (`sudo dnf install ./superawesomemagicalbrowser-*.rpm`,
or `rpm-ostree install` on SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux), the `.deb` on Ubuntu- and Debian-based ones
(`sudo apt install ./superawesomemagicalbrowser_*.deb`).

Built on Firefox 160.0 ([release notes](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/160.0/releasenotes/)).
