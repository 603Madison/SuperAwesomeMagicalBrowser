Your other devices, with SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator.

- **Send to a device.** Right-click a page, a link or a tab: *Send page to…*, *Send link to…* and
  *Send tab to…* list your other devices, the ones that are on first. Pick one and it opens there
  (or when that device is back).
- **Carry on.** The start page shows what your other devices are doing, and your apps, when there's
  something to show.
- **Android:** *Send to device* in the menu, and when you hold a link, hands the page to the
  orchestrator's app, which asks which device.
- Nothing about your browsing goes anywhere unless you choose it from one of those menus.

These show once SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator is installed and signed in (on Linux its agent,
`sam-orchestrator`; on Android its app), and stay out of the way otherwise. To turn them off on
Linux: `superawesomemagical.orchestrator.send` and `superawesomemagical.orchestrator.carryOn` in
about:config.

**Install:** the `.rpm` on Fedora-based systems (`sudo dnf install ./superawesomemagicalbrowser-*.rpm`,
or `rpm-ostree install` on SuperAwesomeMagicalLinux), the `.deb` on Ubuntu- and Debian-based ones
(`sudo apt install ./superawesomemagicalbrowser_*.deb`), the `.apk` on Android.

Built on Firefox 160.0 ([release notes](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/160.0/releasenotes/)).
